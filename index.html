About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering & Computer Science at UC Irvine, where I am supervised by Prof. Syed Ali Jafar .

My research employs applied mathematics and theoretical physics models as essential analytical tools to characterize the fundamental limits and capacity of communication and computation in network computing systems.

I am committed to connecting theoretical insight with engineering practice: designing implementable algorithms and developing practical prototypes for emerging communication and computing technologies.

🎓 Recipient of the UCI EECS Department Fellowship

Research Interests

Information Theory

Specializing in theoretical capacity analysis of network computing systems and the application of emerging theoretical physics methods to understand their fundamental limits.
